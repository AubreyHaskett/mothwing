MothWing

MothWing reads your books aloud.

Open an EPUB, PDF or DOCX and pick one of 10 voices. It runs on your phone, so nothing is uploaded, and there's no account or subscription.

The text highlights as it reads so you can follow along. iOS and Android versions are coming.

MothWing is the sister product to Manuscript Reader, the in-browser desktop version.
